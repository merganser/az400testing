Slide 1: Platform Operations

Speaking Script

Good morning everyone.

As I step into this role, I want to start with how I view Platform Operations and the value it brings to the organization.

For me, Platform Operations exists to enable reliable, secure, and scalable delivery across Data, Analytics, and AI. That's the -mission.

The portfolio represented on this slide is broad. It includes our reporting and analytics platforms, our cloud and data platforms, our operational tooling, automation capabilities, and the foundational services that delivery teams depend on every day.
​​​​​​​

What I want to emphasize is that I don't see these as individual technologies.

I see them as products that provide services to the business.

That means every platform should have:

  • Clear ownership
  • Defined expectations
  • Measurable health
  • Planned improvements
  • Accountability for outcomes

The strategic shift highlighted at the bottom of the slide is probably the most important message I want people to remember.

We're moving from platform support to platform stewardship.

Right now we reactive. Something breaks and we respond.

Stewardship is proactive. We understand platform health, we manage risk before it becomes an outage, we improve reliability, and we create an environment where delivery teams can move faster with confidence.

When Platform Operations is successful, teams spend less time dealing with friction and more time delivering value. Reliability improves. Governance becomes easier. Costs become more transparent. And leadership has better visibility into what's working and what needs attention. 

Slide 2

This second slide is how I intend to turn that vision into an operating model.

The first priority is making health visible.

You can't improve what you can't see.

My goal is to establish clear ownership, meaningful service measures, and a common view of platform health, risk, and operational performance. We need decisions driven by facts and data rather than assumptions.

The second priority is strengthening operations.

That means improving incident response, creating consistent escalation paths, improving observability, and ensuring we have the right operational discipline in place. I also believe vendor accountability is a key part of platform operations. Our partners need to be aligned with the outcomes we're responsible for delivering. 

The third priority is governing and optimizing.

Reliability, security, governance, and cost management should not be afterthoughts. They should be built into how platforms operate every day. My preference is always to use reusable standards and automation rather than creating one-off solutions that become difficult to maintain.

The fourth priority is delivering and scaling.

We will continue enabling new business capabilities, but we need to do so on a foundation that remains supportable and sustainable as demand grows. Growth should not automatically mean complexity. Our operating model should allow us to scale while maintaining reliability and control.

When I think about success, it comes down to the outcomes shown at the bottom of the slide:

  • Faster delivery
  • Higher reliability
  • Lower operational friction
  • Better visibility for leadership

Those outcomes are ultimately what matter.

My commitment is to focus on measurable results, surface risks early, communicate clearly, and always bring a recommended path forward rather than simply identifying problems. That's the culture and operating model I want Platform Operations to represent. 

If there's one thing I'd like everyone to take away from this presentation, it's this:

Platform Operations is not just about running technology. It's about creating the reliability, governance, visibility, and operational discipline that allows the entire Data, Analytics & AI organization to deliver at scale.


That's the direction I'm excited to lead, and I look forward to partnering with all of you as we build it together.
